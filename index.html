Competition Rules

  • Individual entries only.
  • Christmas carol songs only.
  • Karaoke track is allowed.
  • Record in landscape mode with a stable camera.
  • No editing, mixing, or filters.
  • Audio and video must be clear.
  • Christmas setting is preferred.
  • Judges’ decision will be final.
